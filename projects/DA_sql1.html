5. Taux de Rapprochement

Calcul du ratio de couverture comptable et identification des écarts de montant sur ID identique.
SELECT (COUNT(i.trans_id)/(SELECT COUNT(*) FROM external_statement))*100
AS reconciliation_rate FROM internal_ledger i
JOIN external_statement e ON e.ext_id = i.trans_id;
41.34 %

8. Solde Global Final

Écart total de réconciliation (Banque vs Comptabilité).
SELECT SUM(i.amount) as Interne, SUM(e.amount_ext) as Externe,
SUM(i.amount)-SUM(e.amount_ext) as Diff
FROM external_statement e LEFT JOIN internal_ledger i ON e.ext_id = i.trans_id;
